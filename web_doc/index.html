What is TANMA?

TANMA! is a Manifest V3 browser extension for immersion learning. It renders its own learning subtitles over any video element on the page — YouTube, anime streaming sites, or your own local files — and turns every line into something you can read, look up, and study. It's Japanese-first (furigana + rich word look-ups), with secondary support for Korean and Chinese.

In-pageNo separate app — the overlay lives on the video you're watching.
Click to learnEvery word is a look-up: definitions, readings, pitch accent, frequency.
Mine to AnkiSend a full sentence card — audio, screenshot, definition — in one click.
Yours, localYour dictionaries and progress live in your browser. No account.

Why use it?

Because the best study material is the show you actually want to watch.

📺

Learn in context

Read native subtitles as you watch. Words stick because you meet them in real sentences, spoken by real voices.

⚡

Zero friction

No copy-pasting into a dictionary tab. Hold a key and hover, or click — the answer appears over the line.

🈁

Japanese, done right

Furigana over kanji, deinflection back to the dictionary form, multi-word expressions, pitch accent, and frequency — Yomitan-style.

🎴

Effortless Anki

Turn a line into a study card with sentence audio, a screenshot, and the definition — or queue words and batch them later.

Features

Everything TANMA! can do, at a glance.

🎬

Subtitle overlay

A clean, resizable learning-subtitle line over any <video> — even players nested in cross-origin iframes.

🔍

Word look-up

Click or hold-hover a word: definitions from your imported dictionaries, plus Jisho / Wiktionary, with kanji info.

🈁

Furigana & deinflection

Readings over kanji, and conjugations traced back to the dictionary form (勉強したくなかった → 勉強).

🎨

Known / Learning tracking

Mark words Known or Learning; they're colour-coded everywhere so you see your progress at a glance.

📜

Subtitle browser

A side panel of every line — click to jump, bookmark lines, look up words, and one-click sync the timing.

🎴

Anki sentence mining

lapis-simplified cards via AnkiConnect: word audio, sentence audio, still or animated picture, pitch, frequency, glossary.

⧉

Mining queue

Tag words while you watch, then batch-mine them later — muted and hands-off, so nothing interrupts the show.

📥

Subtitle sources

YouTube captions (auto), Jimaku.cc anime subs, drag-in .srt/.vtt/.ass, and LRCLIB synced song lyrics.

🌐

Machine translation

An optional translated second line under the target language (Google keyless / DeepL).

⏱️

Timing offset

Nudge subtitles earlier/later per video, or one-click align a line to the current playhead. Remembered per video.

💾

Persists across reloads

The subtitle file you picked and your mining queue come back after a refresh — keyed to that video.

🎞️

Local media player

Drop a video + subtitle (or an .mkv with embedded subs) into a built-in Netflix-style player.

🎮

Game / VN reader

A texthooker page for visual novels and games, fed by the tanma-hook companion — same look-up + mining pipeline.

📚

Yomitan-format dictionaries

Import the same .zip dictionaries you use in Yomitan — Jitendex, JMnedict, KANJIDIC, frequency & pitch dicts.

How to use it

From install to your first Anki card in five steps.

1

Install & open the dashboard

Load the extension, then open the popup → Dashboard & settings to import a dictionary and set your target language.

2

Open a video

On YouTube, subtitles load automatically. Elsewhere, click ⬆ import to load a .srt/.vtt/.ass file, or search Jimaku for anime subs.

3

Read & look up

Click any word for a definition, or hold Alt and hover to scan a word mid-sentence. Mark it Known or Learning.

4

Queue or mine

Hit ＋ to send a card to Anki now, or ⧉ to add it to the queue and mine everything later.

5

Batch to Anki

Open the queue and press Mine all → Anki. It seeks each line, grabs audio + a screenshot, and builds the cards — muted, so you can look away.

Plugins & companions

Small helper apps that extend TANMA! beyond the browser. Both are in progress — coming soon.

Python · desktop🚧 Work in progress

tanma-asr

Make subtitles from any media

No subtitle file? Drop an audio or video clip onto tanma-asr and it transcribes it to a timed .srt you can import and mine like any track.

  • Powered by kotoba-whisper v2.2 (Japanese-tuned)
  • Fast CPU path (faster-whisper / int8) — no GPU required
  • Drag-and-drop GUI, or python -m tanma_asr transcribe
Python · desktop🚧 Work in progress

tanma-hook

Read visual novels & games

Hooks a game's text (wrapping Textractor) and streams each line to TANMA!'s Reader page over a local WebSocket — with retroactive voice + screenshot capture for mining.

  • Feeds the in-browser Reader (same look-up + mining)
  • Captures the line's voice and a window screenshot
  • Texthooker-compatible line protocol

Companions are optional and will live alongside the extension. They're still being polished — coming soon. ✨
